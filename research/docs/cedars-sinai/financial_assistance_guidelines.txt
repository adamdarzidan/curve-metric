Financial Assistance Discount

The hospital offers financial assistance discounts based on family size and income level relative to the federal poverty level. For charity care programs, patients at or below 100 percent of the federal poverty level receive 95 percent discounts. Those between 100 and 400 percent receive 90 percent discounts, those between 400 and 600 percent receive 85 percent discounts. For uninsured patients, a standard 85 percent discount applies.

For underinsured patients, charity care provides 100 percent discount at poverty level, with 90 percent discount at 100 to 400 percent of poverty level, 80 percent discount at 400 to 600 percent and 70 percent discount for higher income levels. Uninsured discounts for underinsured patients are 60 percent.

For each additional person in the family unit, add to the federal poverty level annual salary calculation. The financial assistance policy provides methodology and additional details regarding discount eligibility and calculation.
